# The best free Discord bots in 2026, and what free actually covers

**Bottom line:** you can moderate a Discord server properly without paying anyone. **Discord
AutoMod** is free and blocks messages before they post, **Sapphire** gives away its entire feature
set including AI moderation, and **Dyno**, **Carl-bot** and **MEE6** all include their automod
engines and their ban ladders at no cost. Paying is for breadth, higher limits, or a detection
mechanism none of the free options have.

We sell a paid moderation tool, which makes this an awkward post for us to write and a useful one
for you to read. It is mostly about the free ones, and our own product appears once, near the end,
clearly labelled as the thing you do not need if the free tiers cover you.

## What you get for nothing, at a glance

| Bot | Free tier covers | Notable free limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Discord AutoMod | 3 preset word lists, 6 keyword rules, spam and mention rules | Preset lists are English only |
| Sapphire | **The entire product**, including AI moderation | AI scans 10 messages per server per minute |
| Dyno | All 19 automod filters, full ban and mute ladder | 3 autopunishments, 14 day mutes, 3 month bans |
| Carl-bot | All 8 automod modules, 11 punishments | 250 reaction roles, 100 weblog entries |
| MEE6 | 8 automod checks, warning ladder, audit log | Enhanced limits are paid |
| Wick | Base anti-nuke, anti-raid, Heat automod | Advanced versions are paid |

## Discord AutoMod, the one you already have

Free, native, in every server, and available for all servers rather than Community servers only. It
gives you the **Commonly Flagged Words Rule** with three ready-made categories, Insults and Slurs,
Sexual Content and Severe Profanity, and a **Custom Keywords Rule** capped at six keyword rules per
server, each holding up to 1,000 keywords and 10 regular expression patterns. On the spam side
there is a **Block Spam Content Rule** and a **Block Mention Spam Rule** with a limit up to 50
mentions.

Its unique property is worth repeating because nothing else can do it: **it blocks a message before
it is posted**. Every bot in this list, ours included, sees a message after it appears and then
deletes it.

**Best for:** every server, as the free floor under whatever else you run.

## Sapphire, the most product for nothing

Sapphire is the standout entry here. Its home page says "completely free" and, unusually, that is
close to literal: reaction roles, join roles, welcome messages, role connections, social
notifications for Twitch, YouTube and TikTok, logging with over 80 log types, moderation cases, and
an auto moderation module in five parts.

That module includes **genuine AI moderation**, flagging insults, threats, identity attacks and
other offensive language with per-category sensitivity. No other free bot classifies message
content. It also attaches extra actions to the Discord AutoMod rules you already have, which
nothing else does.

The paid products are Custom Branding from €5 a month and a Limit Increase plan. The limit worth
knowing about is documented plainly by Sapphire: the AI "only scans 10 messages per server per
minute" without that plan, and full AI language support is English and German.

**Best for:** almost any server that wants breadth and AI moderation without a bill.

## Dyno, the most filters

All nineteen automod filters are free: All Caps, Bad Words, Chat Clearing Newlines, Duplicate Text,
Character Count, Emoji Spam, Fast Message Spam, Image Spam, Invite Links, Known Phishing Links,
Links, Links Cooldown, Mass Mentions, Mentions Cooldown, Spoilers, Masked Links, Stickers, Sticker
Cooldown and Zalgo Text. So is the enforcement ladder, up to Auto Ban and Instant Ban on a
violation count.

The free-tier limits are on policy depth rather than detection: three autopunishments, mutes capped
at 14 days, bans at 3 months.

**Best for:** servers with precise, countable rules they want enforced consistently.

## Carl-bot, the best free responses

Carl-bot's eight automod modules are free, and so is the thing that makes it special: a punishment
set of delete, warn, tempmute, mute, timeout, kick, tempban, ban, message and DM, which **combine
with commas**, so `delete, tempmute 20m` is a single rule. Durations are written naturally as
`3h42m`.

You also get 250 reaction roles, 5 YouTube alerts, 2 Twitch alerts and 100 weblog entries before
any paywall. The `defer` action, which routes ambiguous cases to a channel where moderators vote
with reactions, needs premium.

**Best for:** servers with active moderators who want graduated responses.

## MEE6, free moderation inside a paid product

MEE6's reputation is for paywalls, so the free moderation is worth stating clearly: the eight
automod checks, `/ban`, `/tempban`, `/kick`, `/clear`, the infractions system, immunity roles, the
warning-based escalation ladder and an audit log of around two dozen event types all work without
paying.

What costs money is enhanced limits and the engagement side, levels, economy, giveaways and social
alerts, at £11.99 a month or £49.99 a year **per server**.

**Best for:** servers already on MEE6 for engagement, who do not need to pay for its moderation.

## Wick, free protection

Wick's paid lines are all **Advanced**, **Customizable** or **Increased** versions of something,
and its documentation describes the Anti-Nuke system, Panic Mode, the Restore System and the Heat
automod in full without marking them premium. So the protection is free and the depth is paid.

The exception worth paying for is Smart Backups, given Wick's own warning that without Imaging
enabled a restore "is a finicky process and lacks a lot of information and the server may not be
restored properly".

**Best for:** any server exposed to raids or a compromised admin account.

## Where free runs out

Three places, and they are the same three regardless of which free bot you pick.

**Language.** AutoMod's preset lists and spam filter are English only, by Discord's own statement.
Sapphire's AI fully supports English and German. Every other bot's coverage is whatever word lists
you write, in every language your members speak.

**Throughput.** Sapphire's free AI sees ten messages per server per minute. On a channel running at
sixty a minute that is roughly one in six.

**Media and meaning.** No free option here documents reading what is inside an image or video, and
none of the rule engines classify harassment, threats, scams or self-harm as categories. A calmly
written targeted message trips no counter and matches no list.

If none of those describe your server, stop here. You do not need to pay anyone, and we would
rather say that than pretend otherwise.
