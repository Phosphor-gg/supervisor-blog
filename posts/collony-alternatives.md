# collony.ai alternatives: the honest options for AI moderation

**Bottom line:** on Discord, **Supervisor** is the closest replacement for collony.ai's
moderation, and covers more: 16 AI labels in over 100 languages, image and video moderation, a
free security suite, and about 900,000 messages a month for £13.99 instead of 500 a day for $29.
**Dyno** and **MEE6** are free rules bots if you only need countable filters. Nothing on this list
replaces collony.ai's AI answers or its Telegram support, so if those are why you use it, keep it.

collony.ai figures come from its pricing page, docs and privacy policy, read on 16 November 2026.
Dyno and MEE6 details come from their own documentation, as covered in our
[MEE6 vs Dyno comparison](/blog/mee6-vs-dyno-vs-supervisor). We make Supervisor.

## Why people look for collony.ai alternatives

**The message allowance.** Protect checks 500 messages a day, and its billing docs count one
credit per message. Active Discord servers outgrow that quickly.

**Price.** AI moderation starts at $29 a month for one platform, and both platforms need Grow at
$99.

**Where messages go.** collony.ai's privacy policy names AI providers such as OpenAI as
sub-processors, and it keeps a searchable chat history with logs typically held up to 90 days.
Some communities would rather their members' messages stayed put.

**Discord security.** It has Surge Guard and impersonation checks, but no anti-nuke, join gate,
verification or backups.

## The alternatives

### 1. Supervisor: AI moderation and server security for Discord

Supervisor reads what messages mean with its own self-hosted models, rather than a general
chatbot. It classifies 16 labels including harassment, hate, threats, scams, spam, sexual content
and self-harm, handles over 100 languages, weighs conversation context, and reads images and
video. Message text is not stored.

It also includes a **free security suite**: anti-nuke, a join gate for account age, missing
avatars and harmful usernames, Heat spam filtering, auto lockdown, quarantine, verification and
numbered moderation cases. Panic mode, join raid detection and backups are in Premium.

**Price:** Premium is £13.99 a month, £6.99 a month annually or £4.99 a month over three years,
with £120 of moderation a month included, roughly 900,000 messages at 100 bytes on the Observer
model, across any number of servers. Seven day free trial.

**Better than collony.ai at:** volume, harm categories, languages, video, server security,
privacy, and a REST API with SDKs if you also need moderation in your own app.

**Not as good at:** it does not support Telegram, does not answer member questions, and does not
let you write rules in plain language.

### 2. Dyno: configurable rules, free

Dyno documents nineteen automod filters, from caps and duplicate text to invite links and known
phishing links, all free, with a full mute and ban ladder.

**Better than collony.ai at:** price, and predictable behaviour you configure yourself.

**Not as good at:** it does not read meaning. A calm, well-spelled scam or threat passes every
filter.

### 3. MEE6: an all-in-one with automod

MEE6 documents eight automod checks and a warning-escalation ladder, alongside levels, economy and
social alerts.

**Better than collony.ai at:** engagement features and a free tier with automod.

**Not as good at:** like Dyno, it counts and matches rather than reading meaning.

### 4. Discord AutoMod: built in

Discord's own AutoMod blocks keywords and mention spam, and classifies three preset categories in English, for free. It is also the only tool that stops a message before it is posted.

**Better than collony.ai at:** it is already there and costs nothing.

**Not as good at:** three categories in one language, and no understanding of conversation context.

the honest advice is to keep it for that, and add Supervisor on Discord for moderation and
