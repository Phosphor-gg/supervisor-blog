automod. Pick **collony.ai** if you are on Telegram or want a bot that answers member questions.

## MEE6: the all-in-one

MEE6 is the bot many servers add first. Its automod has eight checks (bad words, repeated text,
invites, links, caps, emojis and mentions, zalgo, and anti-spam), with a warning ladder that mutes
and bans automatically, all free. Around that sit levels, economy, giveaways and social alerts.

It is excellent at what it counts. It does not read what a message means, so a calmly written
threat or scam with no banned word and no link passes straight through.

## collony.ai: the AI community manager

collony.ai puts every message through Surge Guard, impersonation checks, blocklists and then an AI
that enforces your rules written in plain English, with presets for phishing, wallet spam and DM
solicitation. Its impersonation protection, which compares members against your team's names and
pictures, is particularly good, and on Grow it answers member questions from your docs.

Its limits for Discord: Protect checks 500 messages a day, one credit per message, which a busy
server outgrows fast. The AI runs through outside providers (its privacy policy names OpenAI), and
messages are kept in a searchable chat history, with logs typically up to 90 days. And it has no
anti-nuke, join gate or verification.

## Supervisor: AI moderation and security

Supervisor runs its own classifiers, built only for moderation, across 16 labels including
harassment, hate, threats, scams, spam, sexual content and self-harm. It reads over 100 languages,
weighs conversation context, catches creative spelling, and reads images and video. Message text
is processed in memory and not stored.

Its **security suite is free** and uses no credits: anti-nuke, a join gate, Heat spam filtering,
auto lockdown, quarantine, verification and numbered moderation cases. Premium adds panic mode,
join raid detection and backups, plus the AI moderation: £13.99 a month, £6.99 annually or £4.99
over three years, with £120 of moderation a month, roughly 900,000 messages at 100 bytes on the
Observer model, across every server you run.

What it does not do: levels and engagement, answering questions, plain-language rules, or
Telegram.
